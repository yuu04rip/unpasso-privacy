# Un Passo 🌿

**Un Passo** is a private, local-first mobile application designed to help you track your daily mood, personal well-being, and local events while keeping your data 100% secure and private.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Mood & Well-being Tracking:** Log your daily emotions and follow your personal journey in a completely secure space.
* **Local Discovery & Events:** Discover what's happening around you efficiently without compromising your privacy.
* **Reflection & Guidance:** A calm, distraction-free environment for daily mindfulness.
* **Trusted Contacts:** Keep important support contacts close at hand for peace of mind.

---

## 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture

* **Local Storage Only:** All your personal data and mood logs stay exclusively on your device using a secure local database.
* **No Cloud Accounts:** No mandatory registration, no tracking, and no ads. 
* **Minimal Network Requests:** Any temporary data fetch (such as event retrieval) is done securely and handled in real-time without persistent logging or cloud profiling.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

* **Platform:** Android
* **Architecture:** Local-first data handling
